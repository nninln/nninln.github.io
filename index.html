Limiting spectral distributions of random inner-product kernel matrices in high dimensions

I study the empirical eigenvalue distribution of kernel matrices formed from random inner products when the sample size and data dimension grow proportionally. My work combines theoretical analysis with numerical experiments.

Research notes

Marchenko–Pastur law

The law describes the limiting eigenvalue distribution of sample covariance matrices under standard assumptions when the ratio of data dimension p to sample size n approaches a positive constant. When this limit exceeds 1, rank deficiency accounts for the atom at zero.

Stieltjes transforms and resolvents

The normalized trace of a matrix resolvent is the Stieltjes transform of its empirical eigenvalue distribution. Studying the transform gives a way to prove weak convergence of the spectral distributions.
